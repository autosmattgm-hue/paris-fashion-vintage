The boutique

Curated vintage fashion with Parisian discretion.

Fashion Vintage Paris welcomes collectors, stylists, travelers, and loyal clients seeking authenticated designer pieces with character. Each visit is intimate, considered, and led by a boutique team that understands rarity, condition, provenance, and personal style.

Located near Pyramides at 15 Rue des Petits Champs, the boutique pairs archival fashion knowledge with the warmth of a private Paris appointment.
